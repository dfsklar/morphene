Morphene

Paste a short text string and a passphrase. Morphene turns that pair into the same 15-character password every time: mixed-case letters, at least one digit, and a hyphen, underscore, or percent sign.

A site name, account label, or any short phrase. Spaces and punctuation count.

Kept only in this browser tab. Nothing is sent to the server.

Password
